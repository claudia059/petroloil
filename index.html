ABOUT THE COMPANY

TS TRANS OIL LLP

TS Trans Oil is a multifunctional oil storage tank and logistics company in Almaty Kazakhstan, with branches in Houston, Rotterdam and Fujairah. We offer a safe, efficient, and reliable solutions for the storage of a wide range of liquid products like petrochemicals, biofuels, lubricants and other dangerous chemicals.

TS Trans Oil company is built on a foundation of safety, reliability, and customer satisfaction, with a commitment to delivering exceptional service and support to our clients in Asia Europe & America
